About Our Café

Brewing memories & crafting community since 2014.

Our humble beginnings

It started with a simple idea: that coffee should be more than just a morning ritual—it should be an experience. In the winter of 2014, we opened our doors in a small, renovated warehouse with nothing but a second-hand espresso machine and a dream to bring people together.

We spent months sourcing the perfect beans, tasting endless roasts, and designing a space that felt like a living room away from home. Every table, every chair, and every cup was chosen to spark conversation and comfort.

- Eleanor & James, Founders

Crafting the perfect cup

We believe that great coffee is an art form. From the precise temperature of the water to the pressure of the tamp, every detail matters. Our baristas are not just employees; they are artisans dedicated to the craft of extraction.

Partnering with ethical farms across the globe, we ensure that every bean tells a story of sustainability and quality. When you sip our coffee, you're tasting the dedication of farmers from Ethiopia to Colombia.
