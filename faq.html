Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our beans, our brews, and our warm little corner of the world.

The Coffee

Where do you source your beans? expand_more
We partner directly with small-lot farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Costa Rica. Our focus is on sustainable, fair-trade relationships that ensure quality in every cup while supporting the communities that grow our coffee.
Do you sell whole beans? expand_more
Absolutely. We offer 12oz and 5lb bags of all our current roasts. You can purchase them in-store or order them online for delivery. We can even grind them for you if you let us know your brew method!
What is your roasting schedule? expand_more
We roast in small batches every Monday and Thursday. This ensures that the coffee on our shelves is always at its peak freshness, typically resting for 2-3 days before serving.

The Café Experience

Do you have Wi-Fi and outlets? expand_more
Yes, we have high-speed Wi-Fi available for all guests. While we encourage unplugging and enjoying the moment, we have designated laptop-friendly tables along the east wall equipped with power outlets.
Are you pet friendly? expand_more
We love furry friends! Well-behaved dogs are welcome on our outdoor patio. Due to health regulations, only service animals are allowed inside the café proper. We always have fresh water bowls and treats outside!

Menu & Dietary

Do you offer vegan milk alternatives? expand_more
Of course. We offer Oatly oat milk, almond milk, and soy milk as alternatives for any of our espresso or tea beverages at a small additional charge.
Do you have gluten-free food options? expand_more
We have a rotating selection of gluten-free pastries provided by a local dedicated GF bakery. However, please note that our kitchen handles wheat flour, so we cannot guarantee zero cross-contamination for severe allergies.
